## What is VexDial

VexDial turns any crypto wallet into a phone number.  
Call, video, and message anyone — Solana to Ethereum, BNB to a `.vex` name, MetaMask to Phantom.

No signup. No phone number. Just connect a wallet and start.

---

## Features

### Calls
- **VexVoice** — clear audio calls, no camera needed
- **VexVideo** — video with mute, camera toggle, and screen share
- **Interchain** — Solana, Ethereum, BNB, and more
- **Incoming call labels** — see if it's voice or video, and which chains are connected
- **Call history** — missed calls, redial, duration

### Messaging
- Private chat between two addresses
- Send images and voice notes
- Works even if the other person hasn't opened VexDial yet
- Sent → delivered → read status
- Unread badges

### Identity
- **`.vex` names** — register once, call and message by name
- One permanent name per wallet
- Public directory — search and find anyone
- Works with Phantom, MetaMask, Trust Wallet, and more

### Vex Wallet
- Create a quantum-proof Vex wallet inside the app
- Keys stay on your device
- Use it like any other wallet

### Contacts
- **VexBook** — save addresses with names
- Online / offline status
- Last seen
- Call or message in one tap

---

## Privacy

- Calls are end-to-end encrypted
- We never see or store your private keys
- Media stays between you and the other person
- No phone number required
- No central account
- No call recordings on our side

---

## How it works

1. **Connect** any wallet or create a Vex wallet
2. **Dial** an address or a `.vex` name
3. **Talk** — voice or video, encrypted

That's it.
